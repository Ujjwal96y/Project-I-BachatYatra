Our Mission

Building Financially Smart Students

At BachatYatra, our mission is to make financial literacy accessible, engaging, and practical for every student in Nepal. We believe that understanding money should not be limited to finance professionals. Every student deserves the opportunity to learn how to save wisely, budget effectively, invest responsibly, and make informed financial decisions from an early age.

We aim to eliminate the fear and confusion surrounding personal finance by presenting complex financial concepts in a simple and relatable manner. Through educational resources, financial calculators, savings trackers, investment guides, and market updates, we help students develop healthy financial habits that will benefit them throughout their lives.

Creating a Generation of Responsible Investors

Our goal is not only to encourage students to save money but also to inspire them to understand the importance of investing for long-term financial growth. We introduce concepts such as the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE), mutual funds, fixed deposits, budgeting, emergency funds, and financial planning in a way that is easy for beginners to understand.

By promoting financial awareness at an early stage, we hope to build a generation capable of making smart investment decisions, reducing unnecessary debt, and achieving financial independence.

Learning Together as a Community

BachatYatra is more than just a website—it is a growing community of students who believe that financial education should be available to everyone. We encourage collaboration, discussion, and knowledge sharing so that students can learn from one another's experiences and grow together.

Through continuous innovation and valuable educational content, we strive to become Nepal's most trusted student-focused financial learning platform.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every student in Nepal graduates with strong financial knowledge and the confidence to manage money wisely. Our dream is to create a financially aware generation that contributes to a stronger economy by making informed financial decisions, investing responsibly, and embracing lifelong financial learning.

At BachatYatra, we believe that today's students are tomorrow's leaders. By empowering them with financial education today, we are helping build a brighter, more prosperous future for individuals, families, and the nation as a whole.
