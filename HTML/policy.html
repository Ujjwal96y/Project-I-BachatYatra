Our Policies

1. User Privacy Policy

Your privacy is one of our highest priorities. Any personal information collected while using BachatYatra is stored securely and is used only to improve your experience on our platform. We never sell or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes without your consent. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality of your data and implementing appropriate security measures to protect it from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

2. Educational Purpose

BachatYatra is designed to educate and empower students with financial knowledge. The information available on our website, including articles, investment guides, savings strategies, budgeting tools, and financial news, is intended solely for educational purposes. It should not be considered professional financial, legal, or investment advice. Users are encouraged to conduct their own research and consult qualified professionals before making important financial decisions.

3. Accuracy of Information

We strive to ensure that all educational content is accurate, up-to-date, and easy to understand. However, financial markets, regulations, and economic conditions change frequently. Therefore, BachatYatra cannot guarantee that every piece of information will always remain current. Users should verify important financial information from official sources before relying on it for personal or investment decisions.

4. Responsible Use of the Platform

Every user is expected to use BachatYatra respectfully and responsibly. Activities such as spreading misinformation, uploading harmful content, attempting unauthorized access, disrupting platform services, or engaging in abusive behavior are strictly prohibited. Violation of these policies may result in restricted or permanent access to our platform.

5. Content Ownership

All educational materials, graphics, website design, logos, articles, and other content published on BachatYatra are the intellectual property of BachatYatra unless otherwise stated. Users may access and share our content for educational and non-commercial purposes with proper attribution. Copying, modifying, or redistributing our content for commercial use without written permission is prohibited.

6. Updates to Our Policies

As BachatYatra continues to grow, our policies may be revised to reflect improvements in our services, legal requirements, or user needs. Updated policies will be published on this page, and continued use of the platform after such updates indicates acceptance of the revised terms. We encourage users to review this page periodically to stay informed about any changes.
