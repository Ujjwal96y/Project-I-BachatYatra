NEPSE 101 Guide

Author: BachatYatra Team July 2026 12 min read

Introduction

The Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) has become one of the most popular investment platforms in Nepal. Every year, thousands of students, professionals, and first-time investors enter the stock market hoping to grow their wealth. However, many beginners start investing without understanding the basics, which often leads to costly mistakes. This guide will help you understand everything you need to know before investing in NEPSE—from opening your first account to buying your first share and managing investment risks.

Whether you are a student, a working professional, or someone planning for the future, learning about the stock market today can help you build financial freedom tomorrow.

What is NEPSE?

NEPSE (Nepal Stock Exchange) is Nepal's only stock exchange where investors buy and sell shares of publicly listed companies. Companies listed on NEPSE include commercial banks, hydropower companies, insurance companies, finance companies, manufacturing industries, hotels, and many more.

Every trading day, share prices increase or decrease depending on supply and demand. Investors try to purchase quality companies and earn profits through capital gains and dividends.

How Does the Stock Market Work?

Companies raise money by selling shares to the public. Investors buy these shares through licensed stock brokers. Once listed on NEPSE, those shares can be traded among investors.

Prices change throughout the trading session because of buyers and sellers. If more people want to buy a stock, the price generally increases. If more people want to sell, the price usually falls.

Things You Need Before Investing

  • Citizenship Card
  • Bank Account
  • Demat Account
  • Mero Share Account
  • CRN Number
  • TMS Account from a Broker

Benefits of Investing Early

Starting early allows your money to grow through compound returns. Even investing small amounts every month can create a significant portfolio after several years.

Young investors also have more time to recover from market fluctuations, making long-term investing less risky.

Common Mistakes Beginners Make

  • Buying stocks based on rumors.
  • Investing without research.
  • Panic selling during market corrections.
  • Following social media blindly.
  • Putting all money into one company.
  • Expecting quick profits.

Tips for Smart Investing

Focus on fundamentally strong companies. Diversify your investments across different sectors. Keep learning from financial reports and market news instead of relying on speculation.

Remember that investing is a marathon, not a sprint. Patience is often rewarded more than frequent trading.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can students invest?

Yes. Anyone meeting the legal requirements and having the required documents can invest in the Nepalese stock market.

How much money do I need?

You don't need lakhs of rupees. Many investors begin with only a few thousand rupees and gradually build their portfolios.

Is investing risky?

Every investment carries some level of risk. However, investing in quality companies for the long term generally reduces risk compared to short-term speculation.

Conclusion

Financial literacy is one of the most valuable skills students can develop. Understanding NEPSE helps you make informed financial decisions and build wealth responsibly. Start with learning, continue with disciplined investing, and remember that successful investors focus on long-term growth rather than short-term profits.
