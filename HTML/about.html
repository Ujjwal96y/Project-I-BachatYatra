Welcome to BachatYatra

Welcome to BachatYatra, a revolutionary financial platform built by students, for students, right here in Nepal. We recognize that the financial landscape can often seem daunting, especially for the GenZ demographic. However, we are here to challenge the stereotype that young people are not financially savvy.

Our mission is to empower students to take control of their financial futures by providing crucial education on saving and investing.

At BachatYatra, we understand the unique challenges students face when managing finances. With rising tuition costs, living expenses, and financial obligations, it is easy to feel overwhelmed.

Our platform is designed to make financial education simple, relatable, and accessible. Whether you want to save money, understand investing basics, or learn effective budgeting, BachatYatra provides the resources you need.

We believe GenZ has the ability to transform the financial future of Nepal. Through education, community, and practical tools, we are building a generation that understands money and makes smarter financial decisions.
